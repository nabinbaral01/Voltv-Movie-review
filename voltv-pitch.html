Rate it. Roast it. Share it.
The social network for movie lovers — where every rating is a post, every film has a feed, and taste becomes a status game.

The Problem

Movie discovery and conversation are broken across three silos:

  • IMDb / Rotten Tomatoes — rich data, zero culture.
  • Letterboxd — great for cinephiles, but desktop-first, slow, Western-skewed.
  • Twitter / Reddit / WhatsApp — real debates happen, but posts vanish, ratings don't exist, nothing is anchored to a film.

Gen-Z streamers waste hours scrolling streaming apps with no single home to share what they loved, hated, or can't stop thinking about.

The Solution

A mobile-first platform that fuses X-style feeds + Letterboxd-style tracking + TikTok-style discovery.

  • 🎬 Social feed of hot takes & star ratings, every post tied to a film
  • 🎭 Mood + decade + country + Movies/TV/Anime discovery filters
  • 📚 Public Collections that let power users build audiences
  • ⚡ XP, streaks, leaderboards, blue-tick critics — taste as status
  • 🌍 Global catalog from day one — regional cinema first-class

Target Market

Primary: Gen-Z & younger millennials (18–34), active streamers who already post opinions on social media.
Launch markets: India, Nepal, Bangladesh, SEA, US diaspora.
